How this works

Astrocartography maps where each planet was on one of the four angles (Ascendant, Midheaven, Descendant, IC) at the exact moment of your birth. Along a Sun line you shine; along a Jupiter line luck finds you; along a Venus line life softens. The cities ranked above are where your Sun, Jupiter, and Venus lines fall closest. This is a tool for reflection and fun, not a directive: the best place is still the one you love.
